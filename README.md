# ⚡ AI-Based Power Load Prediction System for NCT Delhi

This project was developed as part of the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH)** to address the challenge of predicting electricity demand in the **NCT of Delhi**. The system uses **AI and data-driven analysis** to forecast power load by considering various real-world factors such as weather conditions, solar generation, public holidays, and urban development.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Electricity demand in Delhi fluctuates due to several dynamic factors such as temperature changes, population growth, commercial activities, and renewable energy generation.

This project proposes an **AI-powered load forecasting model** that can help electricity distribution companies make more accurate decisions regarding power generation, grid stability, and energy distribution.

The goal is to **optimize power management and reduce energy shortages or overproduction**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Predict short-term electricity demand in Delhi
* Analyze the effect of weather and seasonal variations on power consumption
* Incorporate renewable energy generation (solar) into load prediction
* Identify patterns caused by holidays and urban growth
* Improve grid planning and energy distribution efficiency
